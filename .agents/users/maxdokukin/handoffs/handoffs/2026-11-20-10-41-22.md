## 6. Design decisions

- **Decision:** The signed-gain abstain selector (MSE on unclipped expected gain, call only when predicted gain > 0) is the main method, with VLM PCA-32 features when frames exist.

  **Alternatives rejected:** calibration, a K+1 softmax, per-tool weights or offsets, pairwise ranking, and flip features (all null or weak on val/test).
